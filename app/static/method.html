结构化 Claim 提取流程A structured claim extraction workflow

将论文中的贡献拆成可检查的观点、证据和结论，并保留来源、边界和未知。每一步都有产出，也有停下来补材料或收窄结论的条件。Turn a paper’s contributions into inspectable claims, evidence and conclusions, retaining sources, boundaries and unknowns. Each step defines an output and a condition for gathering material or narrowing the claim.

这是一套整理与核对的方法。论文可以产生 1–N 条记录，无需满足固定数量；完成提取也不等于结论已经独立复现。This is a method for extraction and checking. A paper can yield 1–N records, with no fixed quota. Completing extraction does not establish independent replication.

一条记录的结构The structure of one record

Claim 是要判断的命题；Evidence 说明依据；Conclusion 是检查证据后能保留的答案。背景可以作为依赖记录，解释可以单独保存，但都要标明角色。Claim is the proposition to assess; Evidence supplies its basis; Conclusion is the answer the evidence warrants. Background can be recorded as a dependency and interpretations retained separately, with their roles marked.

Claim
在指定条件下，哪个对象呈现什么可判断的关系？What assessable relationship holds for which object under which conditions?
Context
对象、条件、比较、测量、关系和量词；保留共同假设。System, conditions, comparator, outcome, relationship and quantifier, retaining joint assumptions.
Terms
关键术语的原文定义、操作方式、来源与歧义。Source definitions, operations, locators and ambiguities for key terms.
Evidence
哪种方法、数据或推导支持它，证据具体在哪里？Which method, data or derivation supports it, and where is that evidence?
Positive
证据实际允许保留的结果。The result warranted by the evidence.
Boundary
对象、模型、任务、参数、测量与假设的适用范围。Applicable objects, models, tasks, parameters, measurements and assumptions.
Negative result
已执行的测试中未成立或反向的结果；没有则记未报告。An unsuccessful or contrary result in a test actually performed; otherwise mark unreported.
Non-inference
不能由当前证据推出的更强结论。A stronger conclusion that does not follow from the evidence.
Unresolved
未检验的问题或未排除的替代解释，并标明作者或整理者提出。Untested questions or alternatives, attributed to the authors or curator.
Provenance
论文版本、原文位置、核对者、日期与状态。Paper version, source locations, checker, date and status.

现有知识库有时将负结果与不可推论共用一个字段。整理时应在内容中明确区分，不能把“没有检验”填成“结果为负”。Some existing library records share a field for negative results and non-inferences. Keep the distinction explicit in the content; “not tested” must not become a negative finding.

CONTEXT BEFORE CONNECTIONS

先明确含义，再判断证据。Clarify meaning before assessing evidence.

我们借鉴形式化数学对对象、定义与假设的明确要求，但不宣称神经科学可以因此变成形式证明。这里的目标是保留经验命题的操作定义、条件与不确定性，让人和 AI 知道可以比较什么、不能推论什么。We borrow the demand for explicit objects, definitions and assumptions from formalized mathematics, without turning neuroscience into formal proof. The aim is to retain operational definitions, conditions and uncertainty so people and AI can see what is comparable and what cannot be inferred.

  1. 1. 找到对象和关系，不先贴主题标签。1. Identify the system and relationship before adding topic labels.

    区分生物制备、记录群体与数学模型；明确是在描述观察、比较、干预还是理论命题。不要将一个模型中的存在性结果改写成大脑机制。Distinguish the biological preparation, recorded population and mathematical model. Identify observation, comparison, intervention or theory. Do not rewrite a model demonstration as a brain mechanism.

  2. 2. 为关键术语回查定义与操作方式。2. Locate definitions and operations for key terms.

    作者所谓“稳定”或“编码”，具体指什么？用什么指标、窗口与分析判断？分别保留概念定义和实际测量，附页码或方程定位。只收录判断这条命题所需的术语，不设数量配额。What does the author mean by stability or coding? Which measure, window and analysis are used? Preserve conceptual definitions separately from actual measurements, with page or equation locators. Include terms needed to assess the claim, without a term quota.

  3. 3. 把判断条件写成一个框架。3. Record an assessment frame.

    对象 → 条件 / 状态 / 尺度 → 干预或输入 → 比较基准 → 测量指标 → 所判断关系 → 量词与共同假设。这个框架帮助阅读，不是替代原文或把复杂关系硬压成单个三元组。System → conditions / state / scale → intervention or input → comparator → outcome → relationship → quantifier and joint assumptions. This is a reading aid, not a replacement for the source or a requirement to compress every relationship into a triple.

  4. 4. 缺口要可见，不补成常识。4. Expose gaps instead of filling them from general knowledge.

    术语状态分为原文明确给出、仅有操作描述、所读材料未报告、有歧义。缺失项为 null 并解释；补充材料没读到，只能说在已提供材料中未找到。不能用教科书定义或 AI 猜测冒充作者定义。Term status is explicit, operational-only, unreported in the supplied material, or ambiguous. Use null with an explanation for missing fields. If supplements are absent, say only that a definition was not found in the supplied material. Textbook definitions and AI guesses cannot stand in for the authors’ definitions.

  5. 5. 再拆分与比较，保留依赖。5. Then split and compare, preserving dependencies.

    只有能分别成立或失败的承诺才拆开；共同假设和完整比较必须保留。跨论文先核对对象、定义、测量、尺度、对照和证据独立性，再提出支持或反驳关系。名称相同不是同义，指标不同也不自动矛盾。Split commitments only when they can hold or fail separately; keep joint assumptions and complete comparisons. Across papers, inspect systems, definitions, measurements, scales, controls and evidence independence before proposing support or contradiction. Identical labels do not establish equivalence; different measures do not by themselves establish contradiction.

一个只用于说明定义问题的例子A hypothetical example about definitions

这是教学假设，不是某篇论文的结果，也不产生数据库记录。This is a teaching hypothetical, not a paper result or a new database record.

“抑制使活动稳定”还不足以判断：是哪个模型或制备？“抑制”是连接权重、药理操作还是细胞活动？“稳定”是动力系统的局部稳定性、放电率方差，还是某段时间内的漂移？“Inhibition stabilizes activity” is not yet assessable. Which model or preparation? Does inhibition mean connection weights, a pharmacological manipulation or cell activity? Does stability mean local dynamical stability, firing-rate variance or drift over a particular interval?

先从原文找到答案；找不到就保留歧义。记录形式可以是：“在［对象与条件］中，相对［对照］，［操作］改变了按［指标与窗口］定义的［结果］。”方括号是待填信息，不能由模型自行补全。Locate answers in the source first; preserve ambiguities if they are absent. A reading frame might be: “In [system and conditions], relative to [comparator], [operation] changed [outcome], defined by [measure and window].” Brackets mark missing information, not permission for a model to invent it.

新提取结果与模板包含这些可选字段。旧记录未被自动补齐；字段完整也不意味着已形式化、已证实或适合自动推理。AI 阅读收益仍需单独评估。New extraction outputs and the worksheet include these optional fields. Older records have not been automatically backfilled. Complete fields do not establish formalization, truth or suitability for automated reasoning. Benefits for AI reading still require separate evaluation.

01 / 提取步骤Extraction step

固定来源与阅读范围Fix the source and reading coverage

记录论文标识、版本、正文和补充材料的可获得性。阅读全文前先区分研究论文、综述与观点文章，避免把引用的背景发现算成本文贡献。Record the paper identifier, version and availability of the main text and supplements. Identify whether it is original research, a review or a perspective, so cited background findings are not mistaken for new contributions.

产出Output
来源清单：DOI / URL、版本、已读部分、缺失部分。Source manifest: DOI / URL, version, material read and material missing.
判断关口Decision gate
只有摘要或元数据时，保留候选状态；依赖未读补充材料的主张暂缓核验。With only an abstract or metadata, retain candidate status. Defer verification of claims that depend on unread supplements.

02 / 提取步骤Extraction step

盘点贡献及其位置Inventory contributions and their locations

按结果小节、图表、公式和附录列出作者实际做了什么。给每项标记背景、直接结果、解释或预测，并记录对应来源。不要按摘要句数或图数决定 claim 数量。List what the authors actually did across Results sections, figures, equations and appendices. Mark each item as background, direct result, interpretation or prediction, with its source. Abstract sentences and figure counts do not determine the number of claims.

产出Output
贡献清单：候选命题、证据位置、角色、是否纳入及原因。Contribution inventory: candidate proposition, evidence location, role, inclusion decision and rationale.
判断关口Decision gate
与本文证据无关的背景不纳入新结果；仍未检查的部分留在覆盖清单中。Exclude background not established by this paper from new results. Keep unchecked material visible in the coverage inventory.

03 / 提取步骤Extraction step

拆成可分别判断的命题Separate independently assessable propositions

每条写清对象、条件、关系和比较对象。若一句话同时声称现象存在、机制成立和普遍适用，要分别检查；若几张图共同支持同一命题，可以归入同一证据包。Specify the object, conditions, relationship and comparator. If one sentence asserts an effect, its mechanism and its generality, assess those separately. Several figures supporting one proposition can belong to one evidence package.

产出Output
Claim 草稿与类型：理论、观察、比较、模型存在性、干预或机制等。Draft claim and type: theoretical, observational, comparative, model demonstration, intervention or mechanism.
判断关口Decision gate
若某一部分可被否定而另一部分仍成立，通常应拆开。共享证据的记录保留依赖关系。If one part could fail while another remains valid, usually split them. Preserve dependencies between records sharing evidence.

具体怎么拆：六个操作How to split: six operations

目标不是把句子切得越短越好，而是形成最小但完整的判断单位。以下是在第 3 步内执行的细化流程，证据强度仍需在后续步骤核对。The goal is the smallest complete unit of assessment, not the shortest sentence. These operations elaborate step 3; evidence strength still requires the later checks.

  1. 先保留原句，再标出它承诺了什么Preserve the source sentence and identify its commitments

    把候选原句与图、公式或段落位置一起保存。逐项标出：研究对象、条件、操纵或输入、比较对象、测量指标、关系方向、量词与强度。尤其圈出“导致”“通过”“普遍”“必要”“足够”等需要额外证据的词。原句缺少条件时回到方法部分查，不替作者补猜。Keep the candidate sentence and its figure, equation or paragraph locator. Mark the object, conditions, manipulation or input, comparator, outcome, direction, quantifier and strength. Flag commitments such as causes, through, universal, necessary and sufficient. Recover missing conditions from Methods rather than guessing.

    产出：原句及来源 + 条件明确的候选表述。Output: source sentence and locator plus a candidate with explicit conditions.

  2. 列出可判断的部分，不按连接词机械切句List assessable parts, not grammatical fragments

    问这句话究竟在回答几个问题：现象是否存在？相对谁有差异？改变什么会影响它？某机制能否解释它？能否推广到另一条件？把每个答案暂列一行。背景、研究动机、实验方法本身和意义解释先标注角色；只有论文实际评估了方法性能时，方法才可能成为结果命题。Ask which questions the sentence answers: does an effect exist, how does it compare, what intervention changes it, can a mechanism explain it, and does it extend to another condition? List each proposed answer. Label background, motivation, procedures and significance separately. A method becomes a result claim when its properties or performance are actually evaluated.

    产出：候选子命题清单；不是最终 claim 数量。Output: a provisional list of propositions, not a final claim count.

  3. 做独立判断测试，决定是否拆分Apply an independent-assessment test

    对每两个候选问：A 成立而 B 不成立，在逻辑和实验上是否可能？它们是否需要不同的对照、假设或推广证据？如果是，通常分开，例如“现象出现”与“由机制 M 引起”。若 B 只是 A 的改写、一个误差值或一个展示面板，不另立命题。数学定理中的联合条件、完整比较中的两组，也不能拆到原意消失。For each pair, ask whether A could hold while B fails, and whether they require different controls, assumptions or generalization evidence. If so, usually separate them, for example an effect and its proposed mechanism. A paraphrase, uncertainty value or display panel is not a new proposition. Preserve joint assumptions in a theorem and both groups in a comparison.

    产出：每次拆分或保留的理由。独立可判断不等于证据统计独立。Output: a rationale for each split or retention. Independent assessability does not imply statistically independent evidence.

  4. 规范化命题，再合并重复记录Normalize propositions and merge duplicates

    用“在条件 S 下，对象 O 相对于 B，在指标 Y 上呈现 R”重写经验候选；理论候选用“在假设 A 下，结论 P 成立”。保留作者量词，不把“在测试配置中”变成“总是”。比较规范化后的对象、关系和范围：若表达同一个结论，合并记录，保留多项证据。不要为了缩短句子删掉必要条件。For empirical candidates, use “Under S, object O shows R in outcome Y relative to B.” For theory, use “Under assumptions A, proposition P holds.” Preserve quantifiers rather than turning tested configurations into always. Merge candidates with the same proposition and scope while retaining their evidence. Do not delete essential conditions for brevity.

    产出：去重后的命题草稿，每条都可单独理解。Output: deduplicated drafts that can each be understood on their own.

  5. 为每条命题找证据，也记录共享Map evidence, including reuse

    给候选逐条绑定证据位置和证据 ID。一条命题可由多个图、数据集或推导共同支持；同一证据也可能支持多条不同命题，但必须显示共享。只有作者讨论、没有对应检验的内容标为解释或假说，不冒充已建立结果。缺材料时暂缓，不用标题或摘要填补证据。Attach source locators and evidence IDs to each candidate. Multiple figures, datasets or derivations may support one proposition; one evidence package may inform several distinct propositions, with reuse explicit. Discussion without a corresponding test remains interpretation or hypothesis, not an established result. Missing material warrants deferral, not substitution with a title or abstract.

    产出：命题与证据的多对多对应表，以及待核对项。Output: a many-to-many claim–evidence mapping and unresolved checks.

  6. 分配主张、结论与边界，确定何时停止Allocate claims, conclusions and boundaries, then stop

    Claim 保存要判断的命题，Conclusion 保存证据允许的回答。不把每一个限制单独计为贡献：未检验范围放 Boundary 或 Unresolved，不能推出的内容放 Non-inference；实际做过且有独立问题的负结果可另立记录。例如“未测试其他物种”不是新 claim，而“在物种 B 的匹配检验中效应反向”可能是。最后确认拆分后没有丢失条件、负结果或共同依赖。Claim holds the proposition to assess; Conclusion holds the warranted answer. Do not count every limitation as a contribution. Untested scope belongs in Boundary or Unresolved, unsupported inferences in Non-inference. An actual negative finding answering a distinct question may warrant a record. “Other species were not tested” is not a new claim; a reversed effect in a matched test of species B may be. Retain conditions, negative findings and shared dependencies.

    停止条件：每条回答一个可判断的问题，有来源或明确缺口，且没有只是换一种说法的重复条目。Stop when each record answers an assessable question, has a source or explicit gap, and is not merely a paraphrase of another record.

快速判断路径Quick decision path

  1. 本文实际提出或检验了吗？否 → 背景 / 外部依赖 / 不纳入本文结果。Does this paper propose or test it? No → background, external dependency, or exclusion from its results.
  2. 只有解释、预测而未检验？→ 标明角色，不能计作已证实结果。Interpretation or prediction without a test? → label its role; do not count it as an established finding.
  3. 包含可分别成立或失败的承诺？是 → 分列候选；否 → 保留完整命题。Can its commitments hold or fail separately? Yes → separate candidates; no → retain the whole proposition.
  4. 与已有候选是同一命题和范围？是 → 合并证据；否 → 保留并检查依赖。Same proposition and scope as an existing candidate? Yes → merge evidence; no → retain and inspect dependencies.
  5. 有足以定位的证据？有 → 进入第 4 步；没有 → 标记待补或解释，不自动升级。Locatable evidence available? Yes → proceed to step 4; no → mark pending or interpretive, without automatic promotion.

假设示例：一句话不等于一条结论Hypothetical example: one sentence is not one finding

以下数字、图号与情境均为教学假设，不来自两篇示例论文。The numbers, figure labels and setting below are hypothetical, not findings from the two example papers.

候选原句：“训练提高模型表现，通过机制 M 实现，并适用于所有任务。”假设实际材料只有：图 A 显示任务 T 中相对未训练对照的准确率提高；图 B 显示相同任务中干预 M 后优势减小；没有测试其他任务。Candidate sentence: “Training improves performance through mechanism M and works across all tasks.” Suppose figure A shows higher accuracy than an untrained control on task T; figure B shows a smaller advantage after intervening on M in the same task; no other tasks were tested.

记录 1：性能差异Record 1: performance difference
在指定模型与任务 T 下，训练组相对未训练对照的准确率更高。绑定图 A；仅限测试协议，不写成所有模型或任务。Under the specified model and task T, the trained group has higher accuracy than the untrained control. Attach figure A and retain the tested protocol, without generalizing to all models or tasks.
记录 2：干预效应Record 2: intervention effect
在同一协议中，干预 M 后训练优势减小。绑定图 B，并检查干预对照与非特异效应；这与记录 1 可分别判断，但不自动证明 M 是唯一机制，也未必证明必要性。若复用图 A 的对照，记录共享证据。In the same protocol, intervening on M reduces the training advantage. Attach figure B and check intervention controls and nonspecific effects. This is assessable separately from record 1, but does not automatically establish a unique or necessary mechanism. Record any shared controls from figure A.
不建立记录 3：普适性No established record 3: universality
“所有任务”没有相应证据。保留原作者表述及缺口，但结论范围收窄到 T；其他任务列为未解决，不能填成负结果。“All tasks” lacks supporting evidence. Preserve the source wording and gap, but scope the supported conclusion to T; other tasks remain unresolved, not negative findings.

反例：图 A 的均值、误差条和另一张重复实验图，如果都只回答同一性能差异问题，通常合为记录 1 的证据包，不生成三条 claim。Counterexample: the mean, error bars and an additional repeat figure usually belong to record 1’s evidence package if they answer the same performance question, rather than becoming three claims.

04 / 提取步骤Extraction step

绑定证据与分析单位Attach evidence and units of analysis

记录方法、数据或推导、对照、测量指标和结果位置。区分动物、细胞、试次、网络初始化与输入批次。数字连同单位、误差定义和原文对 n 的称呼一起保存。Record methods, data or derivations, controls, measures and result locations. Distinguish animals, cells, trials, network initializations and input batches. Preserve units, uncertainty definitions and the source’s description of n.

产出Output
Evidence 包：来源定位、设计、指标、统计或推导假设、数据依赖。Evidence package: locators, design, measures, statistics or derivation assumptions, and data dependencies.
判断关口Decision gate
样本量、误差或独立性没有报告，就明确记为未报告；不自行补齐。If sample size, uncertainty or independence is unreported, say so instead of filling the gap.

05 / 提取步骤Extraction step

校准结论与适用边界Calibrate conclusions and boundaries

先回答证据实际支持什么，再写适用条件。将实际负结果、不能推出的结论、尚未回答的问题分别保存。解释和猜想要标明归属，不能自动升级为实验结果。State what the evidence supports, then its conditions of applicability. Keep observed negative results, unsupported inferences and unanswered questions separate. Attribute interpretations and hypotheses without promoting them to experimental findings.

产出Output
Conclusion：Positive、Boundary、Negative result、Non-inference、Unresolved。Conclusion: Positive, Boundary, Negative result, Non-inference and Unresolved.
判断关口Decision gate
模型能产生现象不等于机制唯一；拟合不是因果识别；不显著不等于证明无效。A model demonstration does not establish a unique mechanism; a fit does not identify causation; nonsignificance does not prove no effect.

06 / 提取步骤Extraction step

逐条回到原文核对Check every record against the source

检查主张方向、条件、证据对应、数字和遗漏。记录谁在何时对照了哪个来源，以及仍有哪些争议。机器可提名候选，人工核对负责来源忠实性。Check the direction of the claim, conditions, evidence alignment, numbers and omissions. Record who checked which source and when, plus remaining disputes. Machines may propose candidates; human checks establish fidelity to the source.

产出Output
核对记录与状态：候选、原文已核对、需更正或待补材料。Check record and status: candidate, source-checked, correction needed or awaiting material.
判断关口Decision gate
source_reported 只表示忠实于来源。它不保证为真，不代表独立复现或作者认可。source_reported means faithful to the source. It does not guarantee truth, independent replication or author endorsement.

07 / 提取步骤Extraction step

粗分类与问题索引Assign broad directions and question indexes

论文保留全部关联记录；claim 归入少量宽方向，并用具体问题组织检索。问“是否已有证据”时同时展示模型、条件和读出，允许回答“目前没有找到”。Keep records attached to their paper, assign a small number of broad directions, and index by specific questions. Answers about existing evidence should show models, conditions and readouts, and may state that none was found.

产出Output
三个入口：方向、论文、具体科学问题，指向同一套记录。Three entry points: direction, paper and scientific question, all referencing the same records.
判断关口Decision gate
未找到证据不等于不存在证据；同属问题族只说明值得比较。Not finding evidence is not proof that none exists. Membership in a question family only signals relevance for comparison.

08 / 提取步骤Extraction step

审查关系并保存修订Review relations and retain revisions

在连接不同论文前核对命题、操作定义、条件及证据独立性。逐条说明为何支持、反驳、收窄、扩展或复现。更正保留旧版本、修改理由和关联来源。Before linking papers, compare propositions, operational definitions, conditions and evidence independence. Justify support, contradiction, narrowing, extension or replication separately. Corrections retain previous versions, reasons and sources.

产出Output
可审计的关系记录与版本记录；证据不足时只保留待比较关系。Auditable relation and revision records; insufficient evidence leaves a comparison pending.
判断关口Decision gate
同文多条 claim 或复用同一数据集都不自动构成独立复现；不同条件下的结果也不自动矛盾。Multiple claims in one paper or reuse of one dataset do not automatically constitute independent replication. Results under different conditions are not automatically contradictory.

不同命题，需要不同证据Evidence depends on the claim type

一句话可以包含多种证据，但每种证据的作用要写清楚。表中是核对要点，不是保证研究成立的自动评分表。One proposition may draw on several kinds of evidence, but their roles must be explicit. These are checking prompts, not an automatic score guaranteeing validity.

命题类型Claim type需要检查What to inspect推论边界Inference boundary
理论命题Theoretical claim假设、适用域、推导或证明。Assumptions, domain, derivation or proof.不能省略关键假设后称为普遍定理。Do not remove key assumptions and present a universal theorem.
经验观察Empirical observation样本来源、测量、分析单位和不确定性。Sampling, measurements, analysis units and uncertainty.相关或解码性能不自动识别机制。Correlation or decoding performance does not identify a mechanism.
比较或干预Comparison or intervention对照、变化量、固定量、混淆与效应。Controls, varied and fixed factors, confounds and effects.局部干预效应不自动等于唯一或普适机制。An intervention effect does not establish a unique or universal mechanism.
模型存在性Model demonstration模型、参数、协议、重复与可检查输出。Model, parameters, protocol, repetitions and inspectable outputs.一个成功配置不等于生物实现或跨条件普适。One successful configuration does not establish biological implementation or generality.
机制或推广Mechanism or generalization区分替代解释的检验，或确实新增的独立条件。Tests distinguishing alternatives, or genuinely additional independent conditions.跨多个图或多个随机种子不自动等于跨任务推广。Multiple figures or random seeds do not automatically establish generalization across tasks.

何时可以结束这一轮提取When this extraction pass is complete

  • 每条命题能定位到具体原文，读者可以回查，而不只找到摘要。Every proposition points to a specific source location beyond the abstract.
  • 每条记录保留条件、证据单位和不确定性；关键术语有原文定义 / 操作描述，或明确的缺口与歧义。Every record retains conditions, evidence units and uncertainty; key terms have source definitions / operational descriptions or explicit gaps and ambiguities.
  • 直接结果、解释、不可推论与未知没有混写。Direct results, interpretations, non-inferences and unknowns remain distinct.
  • 覆盖清单说明已处理和未处理的贡献，不以篇幅短为由隐去负结果。The coverage inventory states processed and unprocessed contributions, without omitting negative results for brevity.
  • 关系有核对依据；机器候选、来源核对与独立复现没有混为一谈。Relations have a checking basis; machine candidates, source fidelity and independent replication remain distinct.

完整记录可以保留“未报告”或“尚未解决”。若核心证据缺失，应保留候选或待补状态，并说明具体阻碍。A complete record can contain “not reported” or “unresolved.” If essential evidence is missing, retain candidate or awaiting-material status and specify the gap.
