# Governance Notes for an Early Pilot

This MVP is a research prototype, not a production publishing service. A public pilot needs explicit governance before accepting real submissions.

## Minimum safeguards

- contributor identity and authorship dispute process;
- conflict-of-interest declarations for reviewers;
- privacy and consent checks for human data;
- protected handling of sensitive locations, vulnerable populations, security-relevant methods, and dual-use material;
- copyright and license verification;
- plagiarism, fabrication, and image/data manipulation reporting;
- transparent correction, withdrawal, challenge, and appeal procedures;
- moderation against harassment and retaliatory reviewing;
- durable archiving and export so records cannot be trapped in one platform;
- no ranking by “impact,” hype, institution, or social popularity.

## Separation of functions

1. **Deposit screening** checks completeness, safety, rights, and machine readability.
2. **Scientific review** checks the claim–evidence relationship.
3. **Community relations** add replication, contradiction, narrowing, and generalisation.
4. **Discovery systems** help users find claims but must not become hidden prestige scores.

## Pilot recommendation

Begin with computational and theoretical work whose artifacts can be openly inspected. Limit the first cohort, manually moderate every record, and publish governance decisions alongside technical development.
