从神经科学开始，把作者提出的观点、关键术语、测量方式和成立条件，连同证据与边界一起保存。不只让人找到精确命题，也尝试让 AI 在明确的对象和条件上比较与推理，而不是只接续一个流畅的故事。Starting with neuroscience, we preserve claims together with their key terms, measurements, conditions, evidence and limits. The aim is to help people find precise propositions and let AI work with explicit objects and conditions, rather than merely continue a fluent narrative.

知识不需要先被证明伟大，才有资格被记录。从计算神经科学与相关领域慢慢开始，一起核对、补充和扩展。Knowledge deserves a record before its significance is known. Starting gradually with computational neuroscience and related fields, we invite others to check, contribute and extend the commons.

CLEARER UNITS, MORE CAREFUL REASONING

在推理之前，先弄清楚我们在说什么。Before reasoning, clarify what is being claimed.

这个项目还有一个起点：形式化数学让我们看到，明确对象、定义、假设与推理步骤，可以让一个命题更容易被检查。我们想把这种对清晰性的要求带到神经科学的文献阅读中，而不是把经验科学强行变成数学证明。Formalized mathematics offers a useful example: explicit objects, definitions, assumptions and reasoning steps make a proposition easier to inspect. We want to bring that demand for clarity to neuroscience literature, without treating empirical science as mathematical proof.

在神经科学中，“稳定”“编码”“学习”等词，可能对应不同对象、指标、实验状态和时间尺度。仅把它们放进同一个主题，仍然无法判断两篇文章是否真的在回答同一个问题。In neuroscience, terms such as stability, coding and learning may refer to different systems, measures, experimental states and timescales. Grouping papers under one topic does not establish that they answer the same question.

01 / DEFINE

先把概念说清楚Make the terms explicit

保留作者的定义与操作方式。原文没有说清楚的地方，记为缺口，不替作者补答案。Retain the authors’ definitions and operations. Record missing definitions as gaps instead of supplying an answer for them.

02 / LOCATE

把命题放回条件里Keep the conditions attached

谁、在什么条件下、相对什么、用什么指标，呈现了什么关系？连同证据与未知一起保存。Which system, under which conditions, compared with what, measured how, exhibits which relationship? Keep the evidence and unknowns attached.

03 / COMPARE

再判断能否连接Then assess connections

先核对定义、测量、尺度与证据独立性，再讨论支持、反驳或复现。同名不等于同义。Check definitions, measurements, scales and evidence independence before discussing support, contradiction or replication. A shared label need not mean a shared concept.

我们的设计假设是：更明确的知识单元，可能帮助人和 AI 更可靠地检索、比较与推理。这个收益仍需检验；结构化不等于正确，提取不等于验证，清晰定义也不是数学 AI 进展的唯一解释。Our design hypothesis is that more explicit knowledge units may help people and AI retrieve, compare and reason more reliably. That benefit still needs testing. Structure is not correctness, extraction is not verification, and explicit definitions are not a complete explanation of progress in mathematical AI.

01 / THE UNIT

不是总结论文。
是拆出可检查的知识单元。

论文保留为来源容器，Claim–Evidence–Conclusion 才是可搜索、可比较、可质疑的知识节点。

01 / CLAIM

论文究竟提出什么？

一句可以被证据支持、收窄或反驳的命题。

02 / EVIDENCE

它凭什么这样说？

方法、数据、理论，以及真正的独立证据单位。

03 / CONCLUSION

结果必须连同边界保存。

Positive实际支持什么

Boundary在哪些条件成立

Negative什么未成立或不可推出

Unresolved仍未回答什么

粗分类负责找到方向；具体 claim 负责回答问题。同属一个问题只表示值得比较，不自动表示复现、支持或矛盾。
